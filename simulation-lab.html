💡 Key Takeaway: Bit-Ordering Conventions

IBM Qiskit lists classical measurement results in little-endian order (qubit 0 is the least significant bit on the far right: |qN-1...q0⟩). In contrast, Google Cirq and Xanadu PennyLane list results in big-endian order (wire 0 is on the left: |q0...qN-1⟩). QuantNexus standardizes both views so you can see that the physical quantum state is identical!

🎲 Finite Shot Sampling Variance

Because quantum measurement projects a superposition into discrete classical outcomes, running with finite shots introduces statistical shot noise that scales as $1/\sqrt{N}$. Higher shots (e.g. 4,096) converge closer to the exact theoretical probability distribution.
